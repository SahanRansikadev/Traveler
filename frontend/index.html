About Traveler

Planning a trip usually means many tabs: one for hotels, one for buses and trains, one for notes. Travello puts hotel booking, transport booking and trip planning into a single flow so you can build a Sri Lankan journey and pay for it once.

Create an account, plan your route, book your stays and transport, and keep every booking in one place.

What you can do

Hotel booking

Compare stays across Sri Lanka, from guesthouses to beach resorts, and reserve a room for your dates.

Transport booking

Book trains, buses, cars with a driver or airport transfers so every leg of the trip is covered.

Trip planning

Choose destinations, dates, stay and transport in one planner and see your full itinerary before you pay.
